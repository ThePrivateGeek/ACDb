Assassin's Creed Unity Guillotine Collector's Case

Game
Assassin's Creed Unity
Year
2014
Category
Collector's Edition
ACDb id
#43

Uplay shop exclusive (now the Ubisoft Store) featuring the Guillotine Collector box with a 41cm figurine of Arno on a bloody guillotine, an artbook, a customised tarot game, a framed canvas print and a wooden music box. The game's Special Edition shipped separately. The priciest Unity edition at launch (€149.95).

Contents

Special Edition of the game (shipped separately), Guillotine collector's box, 41cm Arno on Guillotine statue, FuturePak steelbook case, artbook, soundtrack, map of Paris, framed canvas print, two lithographs, customised tarot card game, wooden music box, two bonus single-player missions (The Chemical Revolution, The American Prisoner)

  • Arno Dorian
