Collected edition of the 4-issue limited series. A return to the Victorian London of Assassin's Creed Syndicate and a tie-in to the Last Descendants young adult novel series by Matthew J. Kirby. Pinkerton agent Tommy Greyling teams up with Evie Frye and Henry Green to hunt a murderer — and a Piece of Eden that could change the world — while in the present day, teenager Sean relives Tommy's memories through the Animus. Written by Ian Edginton. Art by Caspar Wijngaard. Published by Titan Comics.
