Connor's Tomahawk Latex Replica

Game
Assassin's Creed III
Year
2012
Category
Replica/Prop
ACDb id
#676

Officially licensed replica of Connor's tomahawk from Assassin's Creed III, its shape and colours taken from Ubisoft's game files. The blade and haft are PU latex over a fibreglass core, which gives it a realistic weight while staying soft. The haft has a red-banded collar, yellow cord wrapping and a hanging moulded feather on red beads. About 50 cm (19.7 inches) long. Sold by Jinx, which opened pre-orders at $44.99 in September 2012 ahead of the game's launch, and later also stocked by ThinkGeek, Entertainment Earth and, in the UK, Forbidden Planet.
