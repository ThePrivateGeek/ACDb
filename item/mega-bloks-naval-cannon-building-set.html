Mega Bloks Naval Cannon Building Set

Building block model of Leonardo da Vinci's naval cannon, one of the war machines of Assassin's Creed Brotherhood (set CNG11, also numbered 38080; some shops listed it as War Boat). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) as one of its Assassin's Creed Collector Construction Sets. The 214-piece boat has printed blue and gold panels, fabric sails, a working rudder and a turning gun turret that fires. Two micro action figures are included: Ezio Auditore in a red hooded outfit with a cape, and a Heavy Borgia Soldier in gold and blue armour with a war axe. Released in 2015, for ages 10 and up.

Contents

214 building block pieces, 2 micro action figures (Ezio Auditore, Heavy Borgia Soldier), boat with firing turret and fabric sails, sword, 2 hidden blades, war axe
