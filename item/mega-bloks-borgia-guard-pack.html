Mega Bloks Borgia Guard Pack

Game
Assassin's Creed Brotherhood
Year
2014
Category
Building Sets
Type
Mega Bloks
ACDb id
#706

Five micro action figures from Assassin's Creed Brotherhood with a small scene to build (set 94306, also numbered CNF07). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) as one of its Assassin's Creed Collector Construction Sets. The 122-piece pack has an unnamed Assassin in cream robes and a red sash and four Borgia guards. The scene is a stone tunnel entrance, the fast-travel point of Brotherhood's Rome. Halberds, swords and a hidden blade are included, and the Assassin's hood, robe and sash come off. Part of the first wave of six sets, released in late 2014, for ages 10 and up. It was re-issued in 2015 with four figures and 106 pieces (set CNK23).

Contents

122 building block pieces, 5 micro action figures (an Assassin, 4 Borgia guards), tunnel entrance, halberds, swords, hidden blade
