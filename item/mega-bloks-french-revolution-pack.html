Mega Bloks French Revolution Pack

Game
Assassin's Creed Unity
Year
2015
Category
Building Sets
Type
Mega Bloks
ACDb id
#710

Five micro action figures from Assassin's Creed Unity with a small barricade to build (set 94320, also numbered CNC64; Mega Bloks' own site called it the French Revolution Battalion). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) as one of its Assassin's Creed Collector Construction Sets. The 116-piece pack has an unnamed Assassin in a green coat, a brown hood and a red sash, and four French soldiers in blue coats. The barricade is built around a cart wheel, with a barrel, sandbags and a pole that hoists a cloth tricolour on a string. Muskets with bayonets, a sabre and a pistol are included. Announced as a seventh set after the 2014 launch wave and released in 2015, for ages 10 and up. It was re-issued later in 2015 with four figures and 98 pieces (set CNK24).

Contents

116 building block pieces, 5 micro action figures (an Assassin, 4 French soldiers), barricade with cloth tricolour, muskets, sabre, pistol
