McFarlane Toys Golden Age of Piracy Action Figure 3-Pack (Series 1)

Year

Boxed set of three Series 1 Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag pirates: Bartholomew 'Black Bart' Roberts, Edward 'Blackbeard' Teach and Benjamin Hornigold. Each figure is just under 6" tall with around 25-27 points of articulation. Together they come with removable hats for Black Bart and Blackbeard, three swords, six flintlock pistols, two bayonet rifles, Blackbeard's four-pistol bandolier and Hornigold's scabbard. Released by McFarlane Toys in October 2013. Unlike the single-carded figures, the set does not include in-game unlock codes.
