Officially licensed Cable Guys figure of Edward Kenway by EXG (Exquisite Gaming), sold as "Edward Black Flag Controller Holder & Phone Stand". Edward wears his Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag robes with red sash and twin flintlock pistols, standing on a dark base with the Assassin's Creed logo, and holds a game controller or a smartphone in his outstretched hands. About 22 cm (8.5 inches) tall. First listed for 2025, it reached the maker's UK store in July 2026, with other regions following later in the year. SKU CGCRAC400785, EAN 5060525897382.
