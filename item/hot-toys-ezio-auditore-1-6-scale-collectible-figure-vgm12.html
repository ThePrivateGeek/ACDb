Hot Toys Ezio Auditore 1/6 Scale Collectible Figure (VGM12)

Game
Assassin's Creed II
Year
2010
Category
Action Figure
Type

ACDb id
#658

Fully articulated 1/6 scale figure of Ezio Auditore in his Assassin's Creed II robes, number 12 in Hot Toys' Video Game Masterpiece line. Approximately 30 cm (12 in) tall on a TrueType body with over 30 points of articulation, with a hand-painted head sculpt by GOX, sculpted hair with a detachable ribbon-tied ponytail and a tailored fabric costume with leather-like cape, two striped jackets and buckled boots. Armed with a long sword, a short sword, two hidden blades in the forearm gauntlets and six throwing daggers. Announced by Hot Toys in July 2010 and scheduled for November 2010 at 22,000 yen (about US$250).

Contents

1/6 scale figure, 3 pairs of interchangeable hands, long sword, short sword, 2 hidden blades, 6 throwing daggers, necklace, figure stand with Ezio nameplate
