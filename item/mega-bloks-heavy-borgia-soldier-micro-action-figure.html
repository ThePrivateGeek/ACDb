Type

One of the armoured Borgia heavies from Assassin's Creed Brotherhood as a single micro action figure (set CNG89, also numbered 38158). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) as one of its Assassin's Creed Collector Construction Sets. The 36-piece set has the soldier, whose silver and red armour and helmet come off, and a stone weapons rack to build, holding a halberd, a battle axe and a morning star. Released in 2015, for ages 10 and up.

Contents

36 building block pieces, Heavy Borgia Soldier micro action figure with detachable armour, weapons rack, halberd, battle axe, morning star
