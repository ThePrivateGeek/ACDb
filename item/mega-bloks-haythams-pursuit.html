Haytham Kenway on horseback, from Assassin's Creed III (set CNG07). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) as one of its Assassin's Creed Collector Construction Sets. The 56-piece set has a Haytham micro action figure in his blue coat and tricorn hat, with a sword and a pistol, a fully jointed horse with a saddle, and a small scene to build: a hitching post with a water trough and a barrel. Haytham's hat and coat come off. The only Haytham figure in the line, released in 2015, for ages 10 and up.

Contents

56 building block pieces, Haytham Kenway micro action figure, jointed horse, hitching post with water trough, sword, pistol
