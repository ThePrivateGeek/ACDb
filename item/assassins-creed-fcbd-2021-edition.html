The Assassin's Creed FCBD 2021 Edition is an exclusive two-issue one-shot from Tokyopop that ties into both their English publications of Valhalla: Blood Brothers and Dynasty series written by Feng Zisu and Xu Xianzhe, respectively. The book features sneak peeks from the comics, depicting each series' respective first chapters, "An Invitation from England" and "The Flower Banquet (Part 1)".
