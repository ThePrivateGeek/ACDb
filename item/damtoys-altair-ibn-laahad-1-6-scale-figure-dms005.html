Damtoys Altair Ibn-La'Ahad 1/6 Scale Figure (DMS005)

1/6 scale collectible figure of Altair Ibn-La'Ahad from the original Assassin's Creed, sold as 'Altair the Mentor', in his hooded white assassin robes with red sash and leather straps. A new head sculpt with his scarred lip sits on a highly poseable 31 cm DAM muscle body. Comes with a sword and sheath, a dagger and sheath, nine throwing knives with holsters, a hidden-blade bracer, eight interchangeable hands including ring-finger-missing hands, and a stone-tile base with game-logo nameplate. Japanese retail price 30,000 yen before tax.
