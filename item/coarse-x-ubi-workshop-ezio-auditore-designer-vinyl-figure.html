Coarse x Ubi Workshop Ezio Auditore Designer Vinyl Figure

Game
Assassin's Creed II
Year
2014
Category
Statue
Type
Coarse
ACDb id
#657

Stylised designer-vinyl figure of a hooded Ezio Auditore da Firenze in his white robes with red sash, in a menacing stance with both hidden blades extended. Designed by Mark Landwehr and Sven Wasch (coarse) in their sculpting style, which makes the figure look chiselled from solid stone. 14" x 5" (35.5 x 12.7 cm), rotocast vinyl, limited to 200 pieces. Sold exclusively through the Ubi Workshop store from June 2014 at US$399.

Contents

Rotocast vinyl figure in colour-printed box with sponge insert
