Special edition of the Origins comic (#1-4), Aya's story in Egypt and Rome from 44 to 30 BCE, with behind-the-scenes process pages, a cover gallery, creator interviews and concept art from the game. Written by Anne Toole and Anthony Del Col. Art by PJ Kaiowa. Colours by Dijjo Lima. Published by Titan Comics (November 2020).
