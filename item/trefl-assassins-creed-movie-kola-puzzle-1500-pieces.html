Officially licensed 1500-piece jigsaw puzzle by Trefl, tied to the 2016 Assassin's Creed film. Depicts a collage of the film cast including Michael Fassbender's Aguilar de Nerha. Finished size 85 x 58 cm. Marketed in Poland as 'Kolaż' and in the UK as 'Assassin's Creed The Movie'. Trefl reference 26142, EAN 5900511261424.
