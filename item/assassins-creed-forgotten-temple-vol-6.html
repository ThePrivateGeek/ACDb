Volume 6 of the Webtoon series that continues Edward Kenway's story after Assassin's Creed IV: Black Flag. The battle on Sun's ship decides the fight for Macau in 1725 and Madam Lee allies with the Chinese Assassins, while in the present day Noa Kim is pulled out of the Animus. Collects episodes 36-42 of the webtoon. Story by ARC. Art by Tabii. This volume is published in French by Mana Books (January 2026); VIZ Media's English edition has reached Vol. 4.
