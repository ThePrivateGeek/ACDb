Connor Tomahawk Developer Kit

Game
Assassin's Creed III
Year
2013
Category
Replica/Prop
ACDb id
#469

Replica of Connor's tomahawk from Assassin's Creed III, presented in a hinged wooden chest lined with black velvet. It is a different sculpt from the retail Connor's Tomahawk Latex Replica: the blade is weathered and textured with spikes along its edge, the haft is wrapped in dark leather strips, and a white feather hangs on red beads from a natural cord binding. Thought to be a developer or press item; its maker, materials, size and the number made are undocumented.
