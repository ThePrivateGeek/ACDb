A blocky, brick-built Ezio Auditore in his white robes, from Mega Bloks' Kubros line of buildable character figures (set DPH92). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014). The 148-piece figure has a head and arms that move, a hidden blade at the wrist and a metal Kubros coin set into one foot. Ezio was one of the six characters in the first Kubros wave of 2016, alongside Master Chief, He-Man, Skeletor, the Terminator and Spock, and is the only Assassin's Creed figure in the line. Later stock was sold under the Mega Construx name. For ages 10 and up.
