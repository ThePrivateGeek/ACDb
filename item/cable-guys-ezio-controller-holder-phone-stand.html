Cable Guys - Ezio Controller Holder & Phone Stand

Officially licensed Cable Guys figure of Ezio Auditore da Firenze by Exquisite Gaming (now EXG), sold boxed as "Ezio – Phone and Controller Holder". Ezio stands in his white Assassin's Creed II robes with red trim, red-lined cape and Auditore crest buckle, on a white base with the Assassin's Creed logo, his outstretched hands holding a game controller or a smartphone upright. A charging cable runs up through the base so the device can charge while on display. About 20–22 cm (8–8.5 inches) tall, in PVC. Released in 2019 and still in production; the cable included has varied between print runs. SKU CGCRAC300138, EAN 5060525893216.
