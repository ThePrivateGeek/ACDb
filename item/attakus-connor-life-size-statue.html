Attakus Connor Life-Size Statue

Game
Assassin's Creed III
Year
2012
Category
Statue
Type
Attakus
ACDb id
#656

Life-size resin statue of Connor (Ratonhnhake:ton) in his Assassin robes, armed with bow, quiver, tomahawk and pistols, standing on a snow-covered rock base bearing the Assassin's Creed III logo. About 2 m (6 3/4 ft) tall on a 1.3 m wide base and weighing 88 kg; shipped in three crates (122 kg gross). Produced in a very small run by Attakus. First batch delivered from November 2012 at EUR 2,490, and a second batch was sold for June 2013 delivery.

Contents

Life-size resin statue and base, shipped in three boxes
