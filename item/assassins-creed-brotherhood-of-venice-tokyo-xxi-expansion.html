Modern-day expansion for Assassin's Creed: Brotherhood of Venice set in 21st-century Tokyo. Adds playable characters Layla Hassan, Rebecca Crane, Shaun Hastings and Kiyoshi Takakura, along with modern enemies and equipment. Published by Triton Noir.

Contents

Modern-day Assassin miniatures (Layla, Rebecca, Shaun, Kiyoshi), modern enemy miniatures, new equipment cards, new Memory cards, rules supplement
