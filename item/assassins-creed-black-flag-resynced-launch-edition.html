Physical-only launch edition of Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X, released 9 July 2026 at €59.99 (£49.99 in the UK). The game comes in a cardboard sleeve marked "Launch Edition", with a 34-page art book of concept art, character explorations and environment art, a double-sided world map poster with a map of the Caribbean on one side and game artwork on the other, and the Blackbeard's Crimson Pack (Crimson costume, sword and pistol). Sold in limited quantities while supplies lasted, mainly in Europe; Eurogamer reported it would not be sold in the US. Some retailers added an exclusive steelbook (MediaMarkt and Saturn in Germany, JB Hi-Fi in Australia), which is not part of the edition itself.

Contents

Game (PS5 or Xbox Series X) in a Launch Edition cardboard sleeve, 34-page art book, double-sided world map poster, Blackbeard's Crimson Pack (digital)
