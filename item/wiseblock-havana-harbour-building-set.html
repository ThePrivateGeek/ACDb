Building block diorama of Havana's waterfront from Assassin's Creed IV Black Flag, part of WISEBLOCK's Black Flag Series (set HA395106). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by WISEBLOCK, the building-block brand of Alpha Group. The 2,399-piece harbour has a tavern, a blacksmith's shop, a white twin-towered church and a fountain square, with a rowing boat and a jetty at the water's edge. The rooftops flip up and the walls turn to show the rooms inside, and Edward crosses a rope strung from the rooftops to the church. Hidden touches include a treasure chest uncovered by twisting a gear, a secret door that swings round and a hay cart for Edward to hide in. Six minifigures are included: Edward Kenway, Stede Bonnet, Governor Laureano de Torres, Bartholomew Roberts, a blacksmith and a townswoman. The finished model is about 57.7 cm wide, 41.7 cm deep and 31.2 cm tall (22.7 x 16.4 x 12.3 in). Released in the US in June 2026, for ages 18 and up.

Contents

2,399 building block pieces, 6 minifigures (Edward Kenway, Stede Bonnet, Laureano de Torres, Bartholomew Roberts, a blacksmith, a townswoman), rowing boat, 2 instruction manuals
