Attakus Ezio Auditore Deluxe Edition Statue

Game
Assassin's Creed II
Year
2010
Category
Statue
Type
Attakus
ACDb id
#655

Hand-painted cold-cast resin statue of Ezio Auditore da Firenze in the black outfit he wears at the end of Assassin's Creed II, sword drawn, by the Attakus team that sculpted the Black and White Collector's Edition figurines. Approximately 16.7" (43 cm) tall with its exclusive base, which carries a numbered plaque. Sold by Attakus as a cold-cast Deluxe Edition, first edition limited to 200 pieces (ref. C175), in stock in Europe by December 2010 at EUR 475. Sideshow Collectibles distributed it in the US from Q3 2011 at $600.

Contents

Cold-cast resin statue on pedestal base with numbered plaque, Certificate of Authenticity
