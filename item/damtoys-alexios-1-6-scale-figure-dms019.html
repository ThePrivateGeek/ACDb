Damtoys Alexios 1/6 Scale Figure (DMS019)

Game
Assassin's Creed Odyssey
Year
2022
Category
Action Figure
Type
Damtoys
ACDb id
#670

Fully articulated 1/6 scale figure of Alexios from Assassin's Creed Odyssey, about 30 cm tall in PVC and ABS, with a gold crested helmet, red-and-gold cloth scarf, dark brown and gold breastplate, gold left vambrace and gold greaves. Armed with a bow, three arrows and a quiver, a kopis, and a long and a short spear, with seven swappable hands and a display base with the game logo. Damtoys item DMS019, released in 2022 at RMB 1,480 in China; Japanese distributor Milestone later listed it at ¥35,000 for March 2023.

Contents

Articulated figure with cloth outfit and helmet, bow, three arrows, quiver, kopis, long spear, short spear, 7 swappable hands, display base
