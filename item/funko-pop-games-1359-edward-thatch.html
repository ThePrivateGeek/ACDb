Funko Pop! Games vinyl figure of Edward Thatch, the pirate Blackbeard, from Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced. He wears a tricorn hat with a black feather plume over a red headband, with rope fuses hanging from the brim into his thick black beard, and holds a cutlass low. About 12 cm (4.8 in) tall. One of three Black Flag Resynced Pops, with Adéwalé (#1360) and the Pop! Premium Edward Kenway (#1361). Due for release in December 2026 in the US and January 2027 in Europe.
