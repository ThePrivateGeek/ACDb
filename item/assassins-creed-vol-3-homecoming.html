The third and final story arc of the Assassin's Creed: Assassins comic series. The Brotherhood of the Assassins is in trouble. Resources depleted by their long feud with the Templar Order, faced with a world where the goalposts are constantly shifting, they can only struggle to regain their footing. A mysterious collective offers an alliance, but is this third faction a golden opportunity? Or a Faustian bargain? Published by Titan Comics. Collects issues #11-14.
