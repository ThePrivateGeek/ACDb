Officially licensed LED logo lamp by Neamedia Icons designed for Assassin's Creed Mirage. Features the 3D Mirage logo on a Baghdad-inspired base with mosaic-textured backdrop and a sandy-orange glow that evokes the game's setting. Rechargeable battery providing approximately 12 hours of autonomy. Dimensions: 20cm × 8cm. Released alongside the launch of AC Mirage.

Contents

LED logo lamp (20cm × 8cm), USB charging cable
