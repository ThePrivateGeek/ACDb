Mega Bloks 2014 Exclusive Figure

Game

Year
2014
Category

Type

ACDb id

A promotional micro action figure given away free by Mega Bloks in 2014, sealed in a printed bag marked "2014 Exclusive Figure" (item 99708). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) for its Assassin's Creed Collector Series. The figure is a bearded Assassin in pirate dress: an orange hood, a brown coat over a striped shirt and an orange sash, with a pistol, a cutlass and a hidden blade, on a small stand to build. The bag gives no name; fan lists call him Edward Kenway. Mega Bloks announced an event-exclusive figure for New York Comic Con in October 2014, though some sellers list this one as a San Diego Comic-Con exclusive. Never sold in shops. 18 pieces, for ages 10 and up.

Contents

18 pieces: 1 micro action figure with detachable hood, pistol, cutlass, hidden blade, small buildable stand, in a sealed printed bag
