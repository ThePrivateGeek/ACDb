1/6 scale collectible figure of Aguilar de Nerha as played by Michael Fassbender in the 2016 Assassin's Creed film, with a Fassbender head sculpt (unruly hair, beard and facial tattoo) on a 31 cm DAM muscle body and a weathered, movie-accurate fabric costume with hood. Comes with a pair of hidden-blade gauntlets, a bow and arrow, a dagger with sheath, three throwing knives, three bombs, ten interchangeable hands including ring-finger-missing hands, and a rooftop diorama stand with the movie-logo nameplate.
