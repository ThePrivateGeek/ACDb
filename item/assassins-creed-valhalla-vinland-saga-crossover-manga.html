Assassin's Creed Valhalla × Vinland Saga (crossover manga)

Game
Assassin's Creed Valhalla
Year
2020
Category
Comic Book
Type
Manga
ACDb id
#675

A seven-page crossover side story written and drawn by Makoto Yukimura, creator of the Viking manga Vinland Saga, in which Vinland Saga's Thorfinn has an unlikely meeting with Eivor. Made to promote Assassin's Creed Valhalla in Japan, it was posted free on Ubisoft Japan's website on 23 October 2020 and printed the next day in the December 2020 issue of Kodansha's Monthly Afternoon, the magazine that serializes Vinland Saga. Ubisoft also published it in translation, including an English edition on its Assassin's Creed Valhalla website.

Contents

Seven-page manga (Japanese original, with Ubisoft's English translation)
