Mega Bloks American Revolution Pack

Game

Year
2015
Category

Type

Four micro action figures from Assassin's Creed III with a British outpost to build (set CNG10). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) as one of its Assassin's Creed Collector Construction Sets. The 106-piece pack has Connor, with a detachable hood, a tomahawk and a hidden blade, and three British soldiers in red coats, one of them a captain. The outpost has a palisade wall, a barrel and a Union flag on a pole. The only Connor figure in the line, released in 2015, for ages 10 and up.

Contents

106 building block pieces, 4 micro action figures (Connor, 3 British soldiers), outpost with palisade and Union flag, tomahawk, hidden blade, muskets, sword
