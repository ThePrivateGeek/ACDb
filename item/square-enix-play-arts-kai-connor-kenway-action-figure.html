Square Enix Play Arts Kai Connor Kenway Action Figure

Game

Year

Category

Type

Play Arts Kai action figure of Connor from Assassin's Creed III in his hooded white-and-blue assassin coat, with painted skin and weathering detail and articulated coat tails for posing. Painted PVC, approximately 274 mm (10.8 in) tall and about 440 g. Comes with a bow and three arrows, a tomahawk, a sword, two flintlock pistols, three hidden blade parts, four interchangeable hands, hood and hair parts and a Play Arts Kai display base. Japanese retail price 9,800 yen before tax.
