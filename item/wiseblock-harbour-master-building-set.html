WISEBLOCK Harbour Master Building Set

Year

The smallest set in WISEBLOCK's Black Flag Series (set HA395108): a 331-piece harbour master's hut on a wooden jetty, as found in the ports of Assassin's Creed IV Black Flag, with two minifigures, Edward Kenway and the harbour master. The striped roof lifts off, the back wall panels slide open, and the hut is hung with a ship's wheel, an anchor and a painted sign, with a treasure map and compass on the desk. Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by WISEBLOCK, the building-block brand of Alpha Group. Sold on its own and also given free with larger orders from WISEBLOCK's shop. Released in the US in June 2026, for ages 18 and up.

Contents

331 building block pieces, 2 minifigures (Edward Kenway, the harbour master), instructions
