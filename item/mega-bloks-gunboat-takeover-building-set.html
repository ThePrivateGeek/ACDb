Building block gunboat from Assassin's Creed IV Black Flag, the largest set and the only ship in Mega Bloks' Assassin's Creed line (set 94308, also numbered DBJ05). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by Mega Bloks (MEGA Brands, part of Mattel since 2014) as one of its Assassin's Creed Collector Construction Sets. The 580-piece boat has real canvas sails, string rigging and ratlines, deck guns, and two cloth flags to hoist, one pirate and one Spanish. Four micro action figures are included: Edward Kenway, with a detachable hood, and three seamen, one of them in a tricorn hat. Part of the first wave of six sets, released in late 2014, for ages 14 and up.

Contents

580 building block pieces, 4 micro action figures (Edward Kenway, 3 seamen), canvas sails, string rigging, pirate and Spanish flags, swords, 2 hidden blades
