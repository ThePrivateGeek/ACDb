Display box of six building block blind boxes from WISEBLOCK's Assassin's Creed Classic Series 1 (set HA395101). Officially licensed by Ubisoft and made by WISEBLOCK, the building-block brand of Alpha Group. Each box holds one minifigure and a small tower of 90 to 98 pieces to build, with a ledge for the figure to perch on before a Leap of Faith, and a full display box has all six with no duplicates: Altaïr, Ezio, Edward Kenway, Shay, Arno and Bayek. The six builds come to 578 pieces. The blind boxes are also sold singly, at random. Released in the US in August 2026, for ages 18 and up.

Contents

6 blind boxes (578 building block pieces in all), 6 minifigures (Altaïr, Ezio, Edward Kenway, Shay, Arno, Bayek), each with a micro diorama
