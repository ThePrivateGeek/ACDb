Damtoys Bayek 1/6 Scale Figure (DMS013)

Game
Assassin's Creed Origins
Year
2020
Category
Action Figure
Type
Damtoys
ACDb id
#668

Fully articulated 1/6 scale figure of Bayek of Siwa from Assassin's Creed Origins, in a hooded beige scarf, dark brown and gold breastplate, orange shawl, beige apron and red sash, with an eagle-skull necklace. Comes with a bow, three arrows and a quiver, a khopesh, a shield, a figure of his eagle Senu, eight swappable hands and a display base with the game logo. Damtoys item DMS013, revealed April 2020 and released November 2020 at RMB 1,480 in China.

Contents

Articulated figure with cloth outfit, bow, three arrows, quiver, khopesh, shield, Senu eagle figure, 8 swappable hands, display base
