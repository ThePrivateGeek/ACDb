Damtoys Connor 1/6 Scale Figure (DMS010)

Game
Assassin's Creed III
Year
2020
Category
Action Figure
Type
Damtoys
ACDb id
#665

Fully articulated 1/6 scale figure of Connor (Ratonhnhaké:ton) in his blue-and-white hooded Assassin robes from Assassin's Creed III, with a newly sculpted head on a roughly 31 cm (just over 12") DAM muscle body and a tailored cloth outfit. Armed with a tomahawk, a longsword, a bow with five arrows, a hidden blade and two flintlock pistols, with swappable hands and a snow-covered display base. Damtoys item DMS010: first shown at Shanghai Comic-Con in October 2018, officially unveiled in March 2019 and shipped in early 2020.
